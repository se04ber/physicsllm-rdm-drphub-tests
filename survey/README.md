# ErUM C.4/C.5 Survey

A short survey on computing, analysis, workflow and data management
practices, for research groups across the ErUM community. Covers work
packages C.4 (compute & workflows) and C.5 (research data management) in
one form.

Started as a C.4 questionnaire for the C4.M1 milestone. This version also
covers C.5 and asks a bit more broadly, so the answers stay useful for C.4,
C.5, and other agentic workflows across the community.

## Fill it in

Pick a language and download the form:

- [English](https://gitlab-p4n.aip.de/physicsllm/surveys/-/blob/main/ErUM-Survey-C4-C5.en.docx)
- [German](https://gitlab-p4n.aip.de/physicsllm/surveys/-/blob/main/ErUM-Survey-C4-C5.de.docx)

It looks like a lot of questions. Parts are ordered so the ones we learn the
most from come first (A to C, roughly 30 minutes), and the rest takes another
20. Nothing is mandatory. Leave a field blank or write "not applicable"
wherever it does not fit your group, that counts as a complete answer.

## Hand-in

Send the filled-in form to one of us, or open a merge request adding it to
`responses/` in the [survey repository](https://gitlab-p4n.aip.de/physicsllm/surveys).
